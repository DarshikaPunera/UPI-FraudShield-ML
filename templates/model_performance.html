Understanding the Metrics

Accuracy: Overall proportion of correct predictions.

Precision: How many transactions flagged as fraud were actually fraud.

Recall: How many actual fraudulent transactions the model detected.

F1 Score: Balance between precision and recall.

ROC-AUC: Measures how well the model separates the two classes.

Metrics describe the model's evaluation results; live predictions can differ.
